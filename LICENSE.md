## Font

The package name is drawn as vector outlines of the **Arimo Bold** font,
designed by Steve Matteson. Digitized data copyright © 2010-2012 Google
Corporation. Arimo is licensed under the
[Apache License, Version 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
The logo files contain the outlines of the letters only, not the font file.

## Design

The camel's back is drawn from a mixture of two normal distributions, the
model used by splitTypeR to classify the samples. The grey hump represents
the "low-mean" distribution, the red hump the "high-mean" distribution, and
the dashed line the classification threshold (the 95th percentile of the
"low-mean" distribution).

The logo was designed by Astrid Deschênes with the assistance of Claude
(Anthropic) through Claude Code. The design choices were directed and
reviewed by the author.
